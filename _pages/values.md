## 1. The Work Speaks {#the-work-speaks}

**Rigorously tested, efficiently deployed.** Fast progress still needs to be accurate. Quick solutions still need to be robust and adaptable for future problems. I aim to test my methods and understanding. Work is iterative, so I aim to deploy every change efficiently and avoid needless do-overs.

**Slow, simple, accountable.** Slow work helps me stay aware and catch costly mistakes. To me, speed is a strategic choice. Not the default mode. I own my judgment and quality. Wrong turns become lessons. Doing things well the first time, when the context or memory is clearer, saves time later. I aim to keep things simple. If I can't explain something simply, I don't understand it well enough.

**Quality over quantity.** It has never been easier to do a lot without making meaningful progress. I aim to make my work essential, meaningful and relevant. My approach should make meaningful progress toward the solution of the problem. When it doesn't, I change my approach. Showing progress is not my goal. Actually making progress is.

## 2. Humans and AI Complement Each Other {#humans-and-ai-complement-each-other}

**Use AI effectively.** Using AI well means knowing what it does best today. And letting humans do what we do best - holding context, connecting disparate ideas and drawing out diverse opinions. I aim to not just use AI, but to use it intentionally. I aim to use it to improve the quality of what I produce and to get through bottlenecks quicker, while supporting my human strengths.

**Adapt constantly.** AI is evolving rapidly. I aim to keep track of what changes, so it stays a useful complement to my work. I approach this with deliberate open mindedness, to help me use AI effectively and adapt.

## 3. Teams and Community Come First {#teams-and-community-come-first}

**Strong interpersonal skills.** AI has shifted knowledge-work. It is less about knowledge and more about how I navigate relationships - with my team, my community and the world. In the world of AI, interpersonal skills are a competitive advantage. I aim to build strong interpersonal skills, where I learn to bring people together and engage them in productive conversation and problem-solving.

**Psychological Safety.** A team needs to feel safe to belong, generate and express ideas. I aim to help build such an environment in my teams. It lets us spearhead complex and difficult problems. It also lets us stay steadfast and take tough but fair decisions.

**Bold but kind.** Hard problems need bold ideas. I aim to share mine without reservations, but with kindness. I welcome ideas from others the same way. Making a difference demands radical openness and kindness, in equal measure.
